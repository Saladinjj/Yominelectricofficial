## 2. Keyword → intent → buyer problem → category → product → inquiry path

- Buyer problem: joints that loosen, terminals that overheat, panels failing insulation test at commissioning
- Category: Terminals & Connectors
- Product: ym-0209 brass DIN rail terminal block with copper inlet/outlet
- Inquiry path: article → `/products/terminal-connector` → `/contact` or WhatsApp; CTA references terminal schedule and panel drawing, the two documents a real buyer sends

## 3. Cannibalisation check

- `din-rail-terminal-block-guide` covers sizing, rail standards and screw vs spring clamp — selection, not installation procedure. New article is procedural and links back to it.
- `terminal-blocks-connectors-identification-guide` is an identification chart; no overlap with wiring steps.
- `voltage-stabilizer-guide` is a technology overview; `voltage-stabilizer-sizing-guide` is whole-installation sizing; `servo-voltage-stabilizer-guide` is servo-specific. None targets the AC/compressor application intent.
- `fridge-guard-guide` covers refrigerator protection only.
- No existing slug matches `din-rail-terminal-block-wiring-how-to` or `air-conditioner-voltage-stabilizer-sizing`.

## 4. Rejected in this cycle

- Busbar current capacity / busbar size chart: 20 volume, no KD, no intent, Keyword Magic empty.
- Split core CT, prepaid metering, industrial plug/socket, aerosol: demand exists but the intent is already served by existing articles; publishing again would split authority rather than add coverage.
- Aerosol PFAS / GWP angle: PFAS-free status cannot be verified from YOMIN product data, and the claim would be a compliance assertion rather than a technical fact.

## 5. Markets and languages

- EN: global default, US/Europe/Asia-Pacific technical search
- FR: France, North and West Africa — panel building and weak-grid stabilizer demand
- ES: Spain and Latin America — stabilizer demand driven by grid stability, terminal content for panel shops
- AR: Gulf, Egypt, Iraq — air-conditioner stabilizers are a mainstream retail/installer category
