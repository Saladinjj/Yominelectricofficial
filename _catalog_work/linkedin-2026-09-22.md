## 1 — Three-Phase Voltage Stabilizer: Industrial Servo AVR Sizing & Phase Unbalance Control

**HOOK**
When grid voltage drops by just 10%, an industrial three-phase motor draws 11% more current to maintain mechanical torque.

That extra current converts directly into destructive $I^2R$ heat, melting stator winding insulation and tripping production lines.

**PROBLEM**
Chronic phase unbalance and voltage fluctuation in expanding industrial zones.

Factory managers installing precision multi-axis CNC machines or injection molding robots often face fluctuating grid feeds (320V to 440V line-to-line). Worse, asymmetrical single-phase loads in neighboring units pull phase voltages out of balance, causing neutral point drift and high circulating currents in three-phase motors.

**SOLUTION**
Industrial **TNS / SVC Series Three-Phase Servo Automatic Voltage Regulators (AVR)** with independent phase regulation.

Three core engineering advantages:
&bull; **Individual phase regulation:** Three separate microcontrollers and high-torque servo motors independently sample and correct Phase A, B, and C back to 220V/230V, eliminating phase unbalance entirely
&bull; **Buck-boost series compensation:** High-capacity toroidal autotransformers inject or subtract corrective voltage vectors via series buck-boost transformers, achieving >98% efficiency with minimal heat dissipation
&bull; **Stepless ±1% precision:** Continuous carbon roller brush travel delivers seamless, ripple-free voltage adjustment without the electrical noise or harmonic distortion of thyristor chopping

**AND THE PART EVERYONE MISSES**
Direct-On-Line (DOL) motor inrush sizing.

When a 30kW motor starts Direct-On-Line, its locked-rotor current surges to 6x nominal full-load current for several seconds. Sizing a stabilizer to running kW alone guarantees nuisance bypass switching or brush arcing during morning shift startups. Stabilizer kVA must incorporate a minimum 2.5x to 3x multiplier unless soft starters or VFDs are installed.

**KEY BENEFITS**
&bull; Frame capacities from 15kVA to 100kVA (expandable up to 500kVA with SBW series)
&bull; 100% pure oxygen-free copper toroidal windings and series compensators
&bull; Ultra-tight output precision: ±1% to ±3% adjustable (standard ±1.5%)
&bull; Comprehensive protection matrix: over-voltage, under-voltage, phase loss, phase sequence reversal, and thermal bypass
&bull; Tested to IEC 61000 standards with full CE certification

**APPLICATIONS**
CNC machine tool centers &bull; plastic injection molding plants &bull; textile spinning mills &bull; commercial HVAC chillers &bull; printing presses &bull; hospital imaging suites

## 2 — Three-Phase Variac: TSGC2 Variable Autotransformer Sizing & Motor Test Bench Duty

**HOOK**
Variable frequency drives (VFDs) and solid-state SCR controllers can vary AC voltage electronically, but they do it by chopping the waveform.

The resulting high-frequency harmonic distortion ($THD > 25\%$) and dv/dt voltage spikes will corrupt test data and break down motor turn-to-turn insulation.

**PROBLEM**
Electrical test laboratories, motor rewind shops, and calibration bays need an adjustable 0–430V three-phase voltage source with a clean, unblemished sine wave.

Electronic controllers generate severe electromagnetic interference (EMI) that disrupts sensitive measurement oscilloscopes and causes false readings during core loss and no-load saturation testing.

**SOLUTION**
Heavy-duty **TSGC2 Series Three-Phase Variable Autotransformers (Variacs)**.

Three electromechanical design pillars:
&bull; **Mechanically ganged central shaft:** Three matched single-phase toroidal autotransformers stacked on an insulated steel chassis, driven simultaneously by a single calibrated handwheel for perfect phase synchronism
&bull; **Pure sinusoidal waveform ($THD < 1\%$):** True electromechanical turn-to-turn contact regulation generates zero harmonics, zero radio interference, and zero waveform distortion
&bull; **Over-wind 0–430V boost capability:** Commutator tracks extend ~13% beyond the nominal 380V input tap, allowing technicians to boost line-to-line output to 430V for simulated over-voltage stress tests

**AND THE PART EVERYONE MISSES**
The essential role of the star (Y) connection neutral terminal.

While a 3-phase variac can power balanced 3-wire loads without a neutral, connecting the utility grid neutral to the variac’s center terminal is critical for laboratory testing. Without a neutral reference, any load unbalance causes the floating star point to shift, creating uneven line-to-neutral voltages and risking equipment damage on the lighter-loaded phase.

**KEY BENEFITS**
&bull; Power ratings from 3kVA (4A/phase) up to 50kVA (67A/phase continuous)
&bull; Stepless dial adjustment from 0V to 430V AC line-to-line (0V to 250V line-to-neutral)
&bull; High-density copper-graphite carbon brushes with dual constant-force springs
&bull; Precision diamond-ground commutator tracks on Class H (180°C) copper windings
&bull; High operating efficiency exceeding 96% at full load

**APPLICATIONS**
Electric motor burn-in & rewind testing &bull; transformer dielectric & Hipot testing &bull; product compliance testing &bull; university electrical engineering labs &bull; plating & heating control

## 3 — Relay Type Voltage Stabilizer: High-Speed Step AC Regulation for Emerging Grids

**HOOK**
When a utility grid suddenly collapses from 220V down to 130V during evening peak hours, a servo-motor stabilizer takes 1 to 2 seconds to crank its carbon brush into position.

By the time it arrives, the locked rotor inside a refrigerator compressor has already stalled and tripped on thermal overload.

**PROBLEM**
Violent, erratic brownouts and voltage sags in developing residential and commercial networks.

In markets across West Africa, South Asia, and the Middle East, voltage does not drift gradually—it drops instantaneously when industrial loads switch on, followed by sharp 260V+ spikes late at night. Slow mechanical regulators cannot respond fast enough to protect compressors, deep freezers, and air conditioners.

**SOLUTION**
Microcomputer-controlled **Relay-Type Automatic Voltage Regulators (AVS / ACH Series)**.

Three operational advantages:
&bull; **Sub-cycle switching speed (<10ms):** High-speed sealed silver-alloy power relays actuate within a single half-cycle of the AC waveform, boosting voltage instantly before compressor motors stall
&bull; **Ultra-wide 95V–270V input window:** Multi-tap auto-transformer matrix steps up extreme low-line brownouts back into safe ±8% operating bands
&bull; **High B2B volume value:** Eliminates expensive motors and reduction gears, delivering rugged, high-volume electrical protection at an accessible commercial price point

**AND THE PART EVERYONE MISSES**
The 180-second (3-minute) compressor delay switch.

If electrical power flickers off and returns immediately, high-pressure refrigerant remains trapped on the discharge side of the compressor. If power is restored with zero delay, the motor tries to restart against massive mechanical head pressure, drawing stall currents exceeding 30A. The 3-minute delay allows capillary pressure to equalize before reconnecting line power.

&bull; Capacities from 500VA up to 30kVA (wall-mounted and desktop formats)
&bull; High-speed zero-crossing switching prevents relay contact arcing
&bull; Dual digital LED display: simultaneous real-time monitoring of input and output voltage
&bull; Selectable time delay: 3 seconds for electronics / 180 seconds for compressors
&bull; Complete CE and RoHS certified design
