## 6. Method & integrity notes
- **Source:** Semrush (Semrush Guru plan) accessed through the 3UE Tools Share proxy at `https://sem.3ue.co/`, launched from `https://dash.3ue.co/en-US/#/page/m-home`. **Node 4** (Multiplier ×1, GURU regional database) was used, with the **US database** plus per-country database volumes returned by `keywords.GetInfo` (all country databases in one call).
- **Data pull date:** 2026-09-29. Semrush UI shows report date *Sep 29, 2026*, Desktop device, USD currency.
- **Global Volume definition:** sum of all country-database volumes for the exact phrase (matches Semrush's "Global Volume" widget — validated: `motor protection circuit breaker` = 1,780 = widget value 1.8K).
