Mismatched fuse operating curves in motor control centers and distribution panels.

## 2 — Solar Fuse: 1000V & 1500V DC String Overcurrent Protection

**HOOK**
Never install an AC fuse on a solar DC string.

An alternating current waveform naturally crosses zero volts 100 times a second to quench arcs. Direct current from solar panels maintains an unbroken, continuous potential. If an AC fuse opens on a 1000V DC fault, the resulting 3,500°C plasma arc incinerates the combiner box.

**PROBLEM**
Reverse current fault loops in parallel-connected solar strings.

When three or more solar strings are tied in parallel, an internal fault in one string causes the healthy strings to backfeed their combined short-circuit current into the damaged modules, exceeding their thermal limits within seconds.

**SOLUTION**
Dedicated **Class gPV cylindrical fuses engineered to IEC 60269-6 and UL 248-19**.

Three engineering safeguards:
&bull; **Class gPV operating curve:** Clears low-level reverse overcurrents (1.35x to 1.45x In) while interrupting catastrophic short circuits up to 33,000A DC
&bull; **Voltage tier optimization:** 10x38mm format for 1000V/1100V commercial rooftop strings; 10x85mm/14x85mm cartridges for 1500V utility-scale solar
&bull; **Pure silver melting elements:** Precision-punched 99.99% Ag elements packed in dense quartz sand quench DC arcs without carbonizing

**AND THE PART EVERYONE MISSES**
The continuous current sizing multiplier ($1.56 \times I_{sc}$).

String fuses must be sized at minimum $1.25 \times 1.25 \times I_{sc} = 1.56 \times I_{sc}$ to account for bright sunshine irradiance above standard test conditions and continuous thermal loading without nuisance blowing.

**KEY BENEFITS**
&bull; Rated operational voltage: 1000V DC and 1500V DC
&bull; Current ratings from 1A to 32A in compact 10x38mm cartridge format
&bull; DC breaking capacity: 20kA to 33kA at rated voltage (L/R ≤ 2ms)
&bull; High-strength glazed steatite ceramic barrel with silver-plated brass caps
&bull; Certified to IEC 60269-6, UL 248-19, and CE/TUV verified

**APPLICATIONS**
Solar string combiner boxes &bull; central inverter DC input cabinets &bull; decentralized string inverters &bull; commercial rooftop PV &bull; utility-scale solar farms
