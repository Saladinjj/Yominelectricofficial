## 1 — Comb Busbar Guide: Pin vs. Fork Modular MCB Distribution Architecture

**HOOK**
Daisy-chaining flexible cable loops across 24 miniature circuit breakers inside a consumer unit is an accident waiting to happen.

Uneven terminal screw pressure, strand pinching, and localized joule heating turn manual jumper loops into the #1 source of distribution board electrical fires.

**PROBLEM**
Messy, dangerous manual wire looping in residential consumer units and commercial panelboards.

Electricians jamming two or three flexible conductor strands into a single breaker cage clamp create high-resistance contact points. Under full summer air conditioning loads, wire insulation degrades, screws loosen through copper creep, and panels suffer catastrophic thermal runaway.

**SOLUTION**
Engineered **YM-CB Series High-Conductivity Insulated Copper Comb Busbars**.

Three modular distribution design pillars:
&bull; **Monolithic ETP 99.9% pure copper conductor:** Delivers uniform contact resistance and zero ampacity drop across 1P, 2P, 3P, and 4P breaker banks
&bull; **Flame-retardant rigid PVC shroud (UL 94 V-0):** Provides 500V dielectric isolation and complete finger-safe touch protection between adjacent phases
&bull; **Pin vs. Fork terminal versatility:** Precision 17.8mm DIN module pitch with solid tooth pins for universal cage clamps, or notched spade forks for bi-connect screw terminals

**AND THE PART EVERYONE MISSES**
Center-fed load balancing and end-cap safety.

Feeding a 24-pole comb busbar from the central main circuit breaker rather than the far end allows the current to divide equally in two directions. A standard 63A busbar center-fed safely carries a total connected lineup load up to 100A without overheating. Furthermore, fitting molded insulation end caps over cut busbar ends is mandatory to eliminate lethal phase-to-chassis flashovers ($V > 400\text{V}$).

&bull; Fully compliant with IEC 61439-1, CE, and RoHS international standards

Residential consumer units &bull; commercial sub-distribution boards &bull; industrial motor control centers &bull; OEM automation panels &bull; solar PV AC combiner boxes
