## 4. Observations / Actions

1. **`three phase voltage stabilizer` is the single best new seed** — US 40 / Global 490, KD 0%, real CPC ($1.78), and 42.9% of global demand sits in India (210/mo). This is the flagship three-phase AVR/AVS term for the unresearched catalogue area.
2. **`relay type voltage stabilizer`** is the strongest emerging-market play (IN 30 + NG 30 + PH 30 + PK 20 vs US 40) at KD 0% — content should target India/Nigeria/Philippines factory-buyer language.
3. **`three phase variac`** has the highest US volume of the set (70) and a large global pool (510) driven by US + AU + BD/BR; KD is still 0% — worth a dedicated TSGC2 three-phase variac landing page.
4. **`tsgc2 variac` is a zero-volume model-number term** — do not build content around the model code alone; fold "TSGC2" into the `three phase variac` / `variac de voltaje trifasico` pages instead.
5. **`neutral ground bar`** works but the intent is Navigational (US residential/DIY Q&A: "neutral ground bar + kitchen" = 1.3K). It converts to informational blog/busbar-explainer content, not a product page.
6. **`dc power panel`** has the highest CPC ($2.58) and near-max competitive density (0.98) on a thin head term (US 20) — target the `dc power distribution panel` variant (50) instead.
7. **`fire inspection cabinet` returned no data and is off-catalogue** — India volume n/a, no global data; variations point to fire-hose inspection checklists. Recommend dropping this seed and replacing it with a substation DC panel / fire-rated enclosure term.
