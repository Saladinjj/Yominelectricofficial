## 3. Node status, limits & data-integrity notes

- **3UE Semrush node: LOGGED IN and functional.** The dashboard subscription card shows SEO Tools active (111 days remaining at time of run); the Semrush node opened normally and served cached reports.
- **Rate limit hit — metric refresh blocked.** Semrush returned: *"You've reached your monthly limit for updating metrics — You are only allowed to update metrics for 5,000 keywords per month"* (counter showed 5,000/5,000 used).
  - Consequence 1: the **multi-keyword "Bulk" Keyword Overview** (all 8 seeds in one query) returned *"Nothing found"* and required a metric update → could not be used. All figures above were therefore pulled **one keyword at a time** from cached single-keyword reports, which loaded normally.
  - Consequence 2: no metric could be refreshed/built for keywords **not already cached** in the node. `house service cutout` (and `socket distribution box` in the US database) had no cached data and could not be generated due to the refresh limit.
- **No numbers were fabricated.** Every value in this report is copied verbatim from the Semrush UI output. `n/a` means Semrush itself returned "n/a" / "We have no data to show."
- **Scope note:** the 3UE platform displays a notice prohibiting third-party scripts/bots against Semrush/Similarweb; this run used ordinary in-browser navigation + reading of the rendered report pages only.
