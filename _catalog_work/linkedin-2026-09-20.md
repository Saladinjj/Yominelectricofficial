## 1 — Mechanical Lug: Dual-Rated Solderless Set-Screw Connectors

**PROBLEM**
The common mistake in industrial panel wiring is mismatched crimp tooling.

If an electrician crimps an aluminium cable with an undersized copper die, individual strands shear, creating localized hotspots. If they under-crimp with a generic tool, conductor relaxation causes terminal creep, thermal runaway, and electrical fire.

**SOLUTION**
High-strength **6061-T6 aluminium mechanical lugs** with electro-tin plating solve this with calibrated set-screw clamping.

Three engineering principles that guarantee connection integrity:
&bull; **Dual AL9CU certification:** Accommodates both aluminium and copper conductors (250 MCM down to #6 AWG) without galvanic corrosion

## 2 — Test Terminal Block: Safe CT/VT Disconnects for Secondary Metering

**HOOK**
Never open-circuit an energized current transformer.

The moment secondary opposing flux drops to zero, primary current drives the core into extreme saturation, inducing lethal kilovolt spikes across the secondary terminals in milliseconds.

**PROBLEM**
Testing, calibrating, or replacing a 3-phase revenue meter on a live primary feeder.

Without a dedicated disconnect block, technicians either risk life-threatening CT overvoltage arcs or must shut down the customer’s entire primary plant power to perform simple meter verification.

**SOLUTION**
A specialized **3-phase 4-wire test terminal block (TTB)** provides a safe, standardized interface between instrument transformers and energy meters.

&bull; **Sliding shorting links:** Low-resistance copper bridges that short-circuit CT secondary windings ($S_1-S_2$) before the meter circuit is opened

## 4 — DC Surge Protector: 1000V & 1500V Solar PV Protection

**HOOK**
An indirect lightning strike 500 meters away can induce 10,000 volts along a solar DC string in microseconds.

Without a dedicated DC surge protective device (SPD), that transient will destroy module bypass diodes and obliterate inverter MPPT semiconductors in a fraction of a second.

**PROBLEM**
The catastrophic error of installing AC surge protectors on solar DC strings.

Alternating current crosses zero volts 100 or 120 times every second, allowing arcs to extinguish naturally when varistors trip. Direct current has NO zero-crossing. If an AC SPD opens on a DC solar string, the resulting sustained plasma arc burns at 3,000°C, causing devastating combiner box fires.

**SOLUTION**
Dedicated **DC Surge Protective Devices engineered to IEC 61643-31 and EN 50539-11**.

Three core protection features:
&bull; **High-speed DC thermal disconnectors:** Magnetic blowout baffles forcefully stretch and extinguish DC arcs within milliseconds
&bull; **Advanced Y-topology:** Three high-energy MOVs prevent dead shorts between DC+ and DC- even during simultaneous ground faults
&bull; **High discharge capacity:** Nominal discharge $I_n = 20\text{kA}$, maximum withstand $I_{\max} = 40\text{kA}$ per pole (8/20 µs)

**AND THE PART EVERYONE MISSES**
The 10-meter rule for inverter protection.

Per IEC 62305, if the distance between solar panel strings and the inverter exceeds 10 meters (33 feet), separate SPDs must be installed at BOTH ends of the DC cable run to prevent destructive traveling voltage waves.

**KEY BENEFITS**
