Technical Capabilities

What Our Products Support

Core technical features available across our product range.

📋
Multi-Protocol Communication
RS485 Modbus RTU, DL/T645, M-Bus, NB-IoT, Zigbee, PLC, and 4G/LTE options.

Accuracy Class 0.2–1
