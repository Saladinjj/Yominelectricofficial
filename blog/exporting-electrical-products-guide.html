The Complete Guide to Exporting Electrical Products from China

LG
LG Logistics Team 8 min read July 22, 2026

Importing meters and electrical components from China is straightforward when you plan the process properly. This guide covers the decisions buyers face from quotation to delivery.

Certifications and Compliance

Confirm the certifications your market requires — CE, MID, UL, SABS, or local metering approvals — before ordering. Reputable suppliers hold these certificates and will share them with your RFQ.

Samples, MOQ and Pricing

Order samples to verify quality and specifications before committing to volume. Understand the MOQ, lead time and how pricing tiers change with quantity. Factory-direct pricing usually includes customization support.

Customization and Packaging

Specify your branding, voltage, frequency, and communication protocol in writing. Discuss packaging and labeling early — your market may require specific languages or bar codes on cartons.

Shipping and Documentation

Plan for sea or air freight, and prepare the documentation package: commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading, certificate of origin and any test reports. Incoterms define who pays for what at each stage.

Conclusion

Choose a certified factory partner, verify with samples, and nail down documentation. With the right plan, importing quality electrical products is a smooth, repeatable process.
