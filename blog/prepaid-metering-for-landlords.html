Prepaid Metering for Landlords: Benefits and Implementation Tips

ET
ET Engineering Team 6 min read July 30, 2026

Landlords managing multiple units face a familiar problem: reading meters, chasing bills and settling disputes over shared usage. Prepaid metering eliminates most of it.

How Prepaid Meters Work

Tenants buy credit through a vendor or mobile platform and enter a token into the keypad. The meter deducts usage from the credit and disconnects when it runs out. No credit, no power — and no arrears.

Benefits for Property Owners

Prepaid meters remove billing disputes, reduce collection costs, and give tenants control over their own usage. Owners stop subsidizing unpaid consumption and can track recharges remotely where the meter supports it.

Choosing the Right Model

Look for keypad meters with theft protection, a clear LCD of remaining credit, and optional prepaid cards for areas without mobile networks. Multi-rate models support peak/off-peak tariffs.

Implementation Tips

Install one meter per unit ahead of the tenant's main breaker, agree on the tariff in the lease, and provide a simple recharge channel. For retrofits, verify the meter's rated current matches each unit's supply.

Conclusion

Prepaid metering is a low-cost way to make rental power simple and fair. It pays for itself quickly in reduced arrears and administrative time.
