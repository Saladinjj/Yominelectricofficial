The 6 Solar Parts Every PV Installer Stocks (Identification Chart)

ET Engineering Team 7 min read · September 1, 2026

A PV array is only as safe as its DC protection. The six parts on the chart above are the ones installers and EPCs reorder every month: combiner boxes, surge protectors, isolators and DC breakers. Here's what each one does and how to spec it — without the textbook.

The 6 on the Chart

PV combiner box, 2-in / 2-out (1000 V DC)

Combines two strings with fusing and protection in a certified box — the right size for residential and small commercial roofs.

Outdoor combiner box, 4-in / 1-out (63 A, waterproof metal)

The commercial-roof workhorse: four fused string inputs into one feeder, weatherproof metal enclosure for the array edge.

Solar surge protector (T2, 1000 V, 40 kA)

Type 2 DC SPD that clamps lightning-induced surges before they reach the inverter — the cheapest insurance in the whole system.

PV DC isolator switch (IP66, 1500 V DC, 100 A)

The visible, lockable disconnect between array and inverter. Maintenance techs and fire codes both love it.

DC MCB (1000 V, 1–4 P, 10–63 A)

Miniature circuit breaker with a proper DC curve — overcurrent protection you can reset instead of re-fuse.

DC breaker 63–125 A (500/1000 V)

The big brother for main DC feeds and battery lines, 63 to 125 A.

Spec in Four Questions

  1. How many strings? → picks the combiner box in/out count.
  2. System voltage? 1000 V or 1500 V — every part must match.
  3. Where's the lightning risk? → SPD on every combiner and at the inverter.
  4. Who services it? → isolator within reach, rated for the array current.

FAQ

Do I need both fuses and an SPD in the combiner?

Yes — fuses handle overcurrent from string faults; the SPD handles voltage transients. Different threats, different parts.

Can I use an AC breaker on the DC side?

No. DC arcs don't self-extinguish like AC; always use DC-rated breakers and isolators.

Do you OEM-brand combiner boxes?

Yes — your label, your configuration (string count, SPD, monitoring) on our certified platform.
