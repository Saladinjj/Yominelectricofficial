Switchgear Insulation: What Is a Busbar Heat Shrink Tube?

ET
ET Engineering Team October 18, 2026 10 min read

The Dielectric Armor for Compact Switchgear and Arc-Flash Risk Mitigation

Inside modern electrical utility substations, commercial power distribution switchboards, compact ring main units (RMU), and industrial motor control centers, high-conductivity copper and aluminum busbars carry heavy electric currents at voltages from 1kV up to 36kV.

To reduce real estate footprint and equipment manufacturing costs, electrical panel builders engineer switchgear enclosures to be as compact and slim as possible.

However, compact switchgear cabinets force adjacent phase busbars into extremely tight proximity. If bare copper busbars are left exposed, minor airborne dust accumulation, high humidity condensation, or an intruding rodent bridging two phases triggers an explosive, multi-megawatt arc-flash fault that destroys the entire switchgear line.

The Medium-Voltage Heat-Shrinkable Busbar Insulation Tubing (Model MPBS / WRSBG Series) provides the definitive engineering solution: a rugged, radiation cross-linked polyolefin sleeve that shrinks seamlessly over straight, curved, and complex rectangular copper busbars, doubling dielectric withstand, eliminating flashovers, and enabling up to 50% reduction in switchgear air clearances.

Engineering Anatomy: Inside Model MPBS Medium-Voltage Busbar Heat Shrink Tubing

  1. Radiation Cross-Linked Polyolefin Matrix: Formulated from premium polyolefin polymers subjected to high-energy electron beam irradiation, creating a cross-linked molecular network that prevents melting, dripping, or cracking under thermal overloads up to $200^\circ ext{C}$.
  2. Engineered Radial Shrink Ratio (2:1 to 2.5:1): Expands easily over rectangular busbars of varying widths and thicknesses, recovering with uniform wall thickness (2.0mm to 4.5mm) tightly conforming around sharp 90-degree bends without creasing or thinning.
  3. Superior Dielectric Withstand Voltage (≥ 25 kV/mm): High dielectric strength ensures that a single layer of YOMIN 24kV/36kV busbar tubing provides complete insulation integrity against phase-to-phase and phase-to-ground flashover voltages.
  4. Flame-Retardant & Halogen-Free Formulation: Self-extinguishing material compliant with UL 224 VW-1 that extinguishes flame within seconds without dripping flaming polymers or emitting corrosive halogen acid gases during accidental cabinet fires.
  5. Environmental Tracking & UV Resistance: Specially stabilized against ozone, solar ultraviolet radiation, and harsh industrial chemical vapors, preventing surface tracking in contaminated and humid industrial environments.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Busbar Heat Shrink Tube and why is it essential in modern electrical switchgear?

A Busbar Heat Shrink Tube—frequently designated as a medium-voltage busbar insulation sleeve, busbar heat shrinkable tubing, or cross-linked polyolefin busbar wrap—is a specialized heavy-wall, heat-shrinkable insulating sleeve extruded from radiation cross-linked polyolefin polymers. In electrical power distribution switchboards and transformer cubicles: <ul><li><strong>Arc-Flash Fault Prevention:</strong> Uninsulated bare copper busbars inside switchgear cabinets are vulnerable to accidental short-circuits. Intruding vermin (rats, geckos, snakes), condensation dripping from cabinet ceilings, or accidental dropped tools bridge the air gap between adjacent bare phases, instantly triggering catastrophic multi-megawatt arc-flash explosions. Busbar heat shrink tubing forms a seamless, impenetrable dielectric skin that prevents phase-to-phase and phase-to-ground flashovers even under direct vermin contact.</li><li><strong>Switchgear Clearance Reduction (Up to 50%):</strong> Standard electrical safety codes require substantial air clearances between bare busbars (e.g. at 24kV, bare conductors require over 180mm to 220mm of air clearance). Insulating the busbars with YOMIN MPBS tubing allows electrical panel builders to <strong>reduce phase-to-phase and phase-to-ground clearances by up to 50%</strong>, shrinking overall switchgear enclosure dimensions, saving floor space, and reducing enclosure manufacturing costs.</li></ul>

How does Radiation Cross-Linking enhance the thermal and mechanical endurance of Busbar Tubing?

Standard thermoplastic polymers soften, melt, and flow when subjected to temperatures exceeding 80°C to 100°C. YOMIN busbar tubing undergoes high-energy <strong>electron beam radiation cross-linking</strong>: <ul><li><strong>Molecular Matrix:</strong> The electron beam breaks linear polymer chains, creating strong permanent covalent carbon-carbon bonds between adjacent molecular chains, transforming the material into a three-dimensional elastomeric network.</li><li><strong>Thermal Memory &amp; Resistance:</strong> Cross-linked polyolefin does NOT melt at high temperatures. Under continuous full-load busbar operating temperatures ($105^\circ ext{C}$) and momentary short-circuit fault spikes ($250^\circ ext{C}$ for 1 to 3 seconds), the tubing maintains complete mechanical elasticity, zero dripping, and 100% dielectric integrity without thermal degradation.</li></ul>

What is the difference between Busbar Heat Shrink Tubing and standard Thin-Wall Heat Shrink Tubing?

Thin-wall heat shrink tubing is designed for low-voltage (600V) wire harnessing, electronic splices, and cosmetic color-coding, featuring thin walls (0.3mm to 0.5mm) with low dielectric breakdown and minimal tear resistance. <strong>Busbar Heat Shrink Tubing (YOMIN MPBS series)</strong> features a heavy, uniform wall thickness (2.0mm to 4.5mm after recovery), formulated specifically to withstand 10kV, 24kV, and 36kV continuous dielectric stresses, with high tensile strength ($\ge 14\, ext{MPa}$), superior tracking resistance, and puncture resistance against sharp copper busbar punched edges.
