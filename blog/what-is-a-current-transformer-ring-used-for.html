What Is a Current Transformer Ring Used For

ET
ET Engineering Team 7 min read August 20, 2026

A current transformer (CT) ring is a toroidal current transformer that steps high primary currents down to a safe, standard secondary current (5 A or 1 A) for meters and relays. It is used wherever a conductor must be measured without direct connection — from utility metering to industrial switchgear.

What is a current transformer ring?

A CT ring is a current transformer shaped like a ring (toroid), with the primary conductor passing through the opening and the secondary winding wrapped around the core. It produces a secondary current proportional to the current flowing through the conductor, which instruments can read safely.

What is a CT ring used for?

  • Energy metering — feeding CT-operated kWh meters for billing and monitoring.
  • Protection — feeding overcurrent and earth-fault relays in distribution systems.
  • Power quality analysis — current sensing for analyzers and harmonic studies.
  • Load monitoring — tracking consumption of individual feeders or machines.

Solid-core or split-core ring?

TypeAdvantageTrade-off
Solid-core CT ringHighest accuracy and stabilityMust be installed with the cable disconnected
Split-core CT ringClamps around live cables, easy retrofitSlightly lower accuracy class

How to choose the right CT ring

  • Match the primary rating to the load (about 60-80% of maximum).
  • Choose the accuracy class: 0.5/0.5S for billing, 1.0 for general monitoring, 5P10 for protection.
  • Verify the ring opening fits the cable or busbar.
  • Confirm the secondary rating (5 A or 1 A) matches the meter or relay input.
  • Never open the secondary circuit while current flows — short it before disconnecting.

FAQ

What is the difference between a CT ring and a normal current transformer?

A CT ring is a current transformer in a ring (toroidal) form, designed to clamp around a cable or busbar. 'Ring' refers to the shape; the working principle is the same as any current transformer.

Can I install a CT ring without cutting the cable?

Yes. Split-core CT rings open and clamp around an existing cable without disconnecting it, which is why they are ideal for retrofits.

What size CT ring do I need?

The ring opening must fit the cable or busbar, and the primary rating should be about 60-80% of the maximum expected load for accurate metering.

Final Thoughts

Current transformer rings are the safe, practical way to measure high currents — for billing, protection, or analysis. Choosing the right rating, class, and form factor keeps both people and data safe. Start with Yomin Electric’s CT range.
