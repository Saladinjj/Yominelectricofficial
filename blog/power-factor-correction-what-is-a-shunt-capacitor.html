Power Factor Correction: What Is a Shunt Capacitor?

ET
ET Engineering Team October 11, 2026 10 min read

The Reactive Power Workhorse of Industrial & Commercial Energy Efficiency

In manufacturing plants, industrial processing facilities, commercial high-rise towers, and electrical distribution substations, electrical loads are dominated by inductive equipment: heavy three-phase AC induction motors, refrigeration compressors, welding machines, and power distribution transformers.

To establish the alternating magnetic fields essential for operation, these inductive machines continuously draw massive amounts of lagging reactive power (measured in kilovars or kvar).

Because reactive power does no productive work but consumes distribution capacity, it drives facility power factor down—often to 0.70 to 0.82. Electrical utilities worldwide penalize low power factor with massive commercial billing surcharges, while facilities suffer from overheated transformers, tripping circuit breakers, and severe line voltage drop.

The Low-Voltage Self-Healing Shunt Power Capacitor (Model BSMJ Series) provides the universal engineering remedy: high-capacitance static electrical devices connected in parallel across the distribution bus to generate leading reactive power on-site, immediately elevating system power factor above 0.95 to 0.98 and eliminating utility penalties.

Engineering Anatomy: Inside a Model BSMJ Self-Healing Shunt Power Capacitor

  1. Zinc-Aluminium Vacuum-Metallized Polypropylene Dielectric: Pinhole-free dielectric film delivers exceptional self-healing properties that instantly clear microscopic transient voltage breakdowns without short-circuiting.
  2. All-Phase Mechanical Overpressure Tear-Off Disconnector: Engineered expanding bellows lid pushes upward under internal gas pressure, mechanically severing internal connections to prevent container rupture.
  3. Integrated Ceramic Bleeder Discharge Resistors: Built-in ceramic resistor assembly rapidly discharges residual capacitor terminal voltage from 450V down below safe 50V within 3 minutes of disconnection.
  4. Dry-Type Non-Toxic Resin Fill: 100% free of hazardous polychlorinated biphenyls (PCBs) or flammable dielectric oils; filled with flame-retardant polyurethane resin ensuring zero leakage in any mounting orientation.
  5. Seamless Extruded Cylindrical Aluminium Can: Heavy-gauge aluminium casing provides superior heat dissipation, corrosion resistance, and an integrated bottom M12 threaded stud for structural mounting and equipment grounding.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Shunt Capacitor and how does it improve Power Factor (PF) in electrical distribution networks?

A Shunt Capacitor—widely designated as a power factor correction capacitor, shunt power capacitor, or VAR compensator—is a specialized high-capacitance static electrical device connected in parallel (shunt) across an AC electrical distribution bus. In industrial manufacturing facilities, commercial HVAC chiller rooms, and power networks: <ul><li><strong>The Lagging Reactive Power Problem:</strong> Inductive equipment (such as AC induction motors, power transformers, welders, and induction heaters) requires alternating magnetic fields to operate. This creates <strong>lagging inductive reactive current</strong> where the current waveform lags behind the voltage waveform, causing the facility's Power Factor (PF = kW / kVA) to drop from unity (1.0) down to poor levels such as 0.70 or 0.80. This forces electrical utilities to supply massive reactive current, leading to severe utility penalty fees, cable overheating, and line voltage drop.</li><li><strong>The Shunt Capacitor Solution:</strong> A shunt capacitor operates with leading reactive current (current leads voltage by 90 degrees). When placed in parallel across the load, the capacitor acts as a localized generator of reactive power (kvar), supplying the magnetizing reactive energy directly on-site. The utility grid now supplies only active work-producing real power (kW), instantly restoring the Power Factor to <strong>0.95–0.98</strong> and eliminating utility billing penalties.</li></ul>

What is the "Self-Healing" mechanism in metallized polypropylene power capacitors?

In traditional paper-in-oil capacitors, a dielectric breakdown caused by a transient overvoltage surge created a permanent short-circuit path, causing the capacitor to explode or blow fuses. YOMIN BSMJ Capacitors feature advanced <strong>vacuum-metallized polypropylene film</strong>: <ul><li><strong>Microscopic Vaporization:</strong> The conductive electrodes consist of a microscopically thin layer of zinc-aluminium alloy (a few nanometers thick) deposited directly onto the plastic film.</li><li><strong>Self-Clearing Action:</strong> If a high-voltage spike punctures a localized weak spot in the dielectric, the micro-arc vaporizes the ultra-thin metallic layer around the puncture point in microseconds ($< 10$ &mu;s).</li><li><strong>Instant Restoration:</strong> The vaporized metal clears away, leaving an insulating dielectric moat around the fault. The capacitor continues operating normally with negligible capacitance loss ($< 0.001\%$) without tripping circuit breakers or catching fire.</li></ul>

How does the internal overpressure tear-off safety disconnector prevent container explosion during end-of-life failures?

At the end of a capacitor's 100,000-hour service life, multiple dielectric breakdowns eventually generate internal gas pressure. To prevent catastrophic case rupture, YOMIN BSMJ cylindrical capacitors incorporate an engineered <strong>all-phase mechanical overpressure disconnector</strong>. The cylindrical aluminium can features flexible accordion-pleated expansion grooves near the top lid. As internal gas pressure builds up, the lid pushes upward axially by 10 to 15 mm. This upward expansion mechanically pulls and snaps the internal lead wires at pre-notched neck points, disconnecting all three internal phases simultaneously and permanently isolating the unit from the power grid before the container can burst.
