Terminal Blocks, Lugs & Bars: The Identification Chart for Wiring Pros

ET Engineering Team 6 min read · September 1, 2026

Loose connections cause a shocking share of electrical fires — and the fix is usually a few cents of the right hardware. The chart above maps the six terminal and connector families we manufacture: DIN rail blocks, neutral/ground bars, mechanical lugs and cable connectors. Here's the quick tour.

The 6 on the Chart

DIN rail terminal block (brass, copper I/O)

The panel builder's staple: brass conductor with copper inlet/outlet in a PA body, clips onto standard DIN rail. Clean, serviceable, professional wiring.

Plastic terminal block (brass/PC)

General-purpose connection block for appliances and junction wiring — the high-volume consumable every electrician keeps in the van.

Neutral / ground bar (aluminium, link lug)

The organised way to terminate neutrals and earths in a panel: screw terminals on an aluminium bar with mounting tabs. Blue/green coding keeps grounding schemes clean.

Mechanical lug TA-250 (250 mcm–6)

Heavy aluminium screw-terminal splice connector for big conductors — the utility and feeder workhorse, easy installation, no crimp tool needed.

Single-hole aluminium lug

One conductor in, one bolt hole out — terminating power cables to busbars and equipment pads.

Multi-hole aluminium-copper lug

Multiple wire entry holes for flexible fitting — when one termination point gathers several conductors.

Picking the Right Part in 30 Seconds

  • Panel wiring that must stay serviceable? DIN rail terminal blocks.
  • Neutrals and earths? Neutral/ground bars — never daisy-chain grounds under one screw.
  • Big cable splices without a crimp tool? Mechanical lugs (TA-250 style).
  • Cable-to-busbar terminations? Single or multi-hole lugs, sized to the conductor AWG/mm².

FAQ

Brass or aluminium for lugs?

Brass for general copper wiring; aluminium mechanical lugs for aluminium conductors and large splices — match the lug metal to the conductor to avoid galvanic corrosion.

Can you make custom bars and hole patterns?

Yes — custom hole counts, lengths and markings are routine OEM orders.

Are DIN rail blocks sold per piece or per pack?

We quote per your packaging need — bulk cartons for manufacturers or retail packs for distributors.
