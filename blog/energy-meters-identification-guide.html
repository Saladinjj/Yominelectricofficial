Energy Meters, Decoded: The 9 Types Every Buyer Should Know

ET Engineering Team 8 min read · September 1, 2026

Let's be honest: type "energy meter" into a sourcing platform and you get a wall of look-alike white boxes. Single phase, three phase, DIN rail, prepaid, smart, mechanical — the names blur together, and ordering the wrong one is an expensive way to learn the difference.

So we made a chart. The identification chart above shows the nine meter families we manufacture every day, with the parts that actually matter labelled: terminals, displays, communication ports, card slots. Here's the same chart in plain words, so you can match your project to the right box in five minutes.

The 9 Meters on the Chart, in Plain English

1. Single phase mini DIN rail kWh meter

The landlord's favourite. One module wide, clips onto a DIN rail, counts kWh on a small LCD. Perfect for sub-metering flats, shops and tenant units — cheap, simple, honest.

2. Three phase WiFi smart meter

The connected one. 7-terminal block, relay output for remote disconnect, WiFi module and app control. If you bill tenants remotely or manage sites from a phone, this is your meter.

3. Prepaid energy meter

Card slot, keypad, tamper-proof body. The customer pays first, then uses power. Utilities and landlords in Africa, South Asia and Latin America run whole portfolios on these — no more chasing bills.

4. Single phase LCD energy meter

The household workhorse: 220 V, 5(60) A, clear LCD, high-precision measurement. The meter most distributors keep on the shelf because it always sells.

5. Three phase rail-mounted meter

4-pole LCD with backlight and a transparent cover, built for panels. When a factory or commercial building needs per-feeder metering without cutting panel space, this is the answer.

6. Three phase M-Bus infrared kWh meter

The utility-grade communicator: M-Bus interface, infrared port, CT included. Made for buildings where meters feed a central billing or BMS system.

7. Mechanical kWh meter

The classic spinning-dial meter with a bakelite cover. Still in huge demand across markets that value a meter that works for 20 years with zero electronics.

8. Energy monitoring meter

A single-phase meter built for monitoring systems — the one you specify when the job is visibility, not just billing.

9. Anti-tamper smart meter

Tamper detection, sealed design, smart reading. For utilities and landlords who are tired of "mysterious" losses between the main meter and the sum of the sub-meters.

FAQ

Can I mix mechanical and digital meters in one project?

Yes — they measure independently. Many distributors sell mechanical meters for basic households and digital/prepaid for commercial units in the same development.

WiFi models support app control, and M-Bus/RS485 models integrate with common billing and BMS platforms. Tell us your system and we'll confirm compatibility before you order.
