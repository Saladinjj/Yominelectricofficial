Power Quality & Safety: What Is an Isolation Transformer?

ET
ET Engineering Team October 18, 2026 10 min read

The Galvanic Electrical Firewall for Modern Industrial and Medical Infrastructure

Inside modern manufacturing plants, hospital surgical suites, commercial data centers, and advanced automation facilities, electronic power supplies, microprocessor PLCs, and medical monitors operate around the clock.

However, modern electrical distribution grids are flooded with high-frequency switching harmonics from solar inverters, massive voltage spikes from lightning strikes, and common-mode electrical noise generated by Variable Frequency Drives (VFDs).

Furthermore, in large commercial buildings, ground potential differences between distant utility grounding rods create continuous circulating ground loop currents that induce phantom voltages, distort instrumentation sensor readings, and destroy delicate silicon chips.

The Dry-Type Galvanic Isolation Transformer (Model SG / DG Series) provides the definitive engineering firewall: a precision power transformer that magnetically transfers energy across a high-dielectric physical air gap while completely isolating the input and output circuits, eliminating ground loops, filtering electrical transients, and protecting human operators from lethal electric shock.

Engineering Anatomy: Inside a Model SG Three-Phase Dry-Type Isolation Transformer

  1. Physically Separated Primary and Secondary Copper Coils: Precision-wound using high-purity electrolytic copper magnet wire (Cu-ETP 99.9%). Primary and secondary windings are separated by heavy-gauge high-temperature Nomex / mica barrier insulation tested to withstand 3000V AC.
  2. Grounded Electrostatic Faraday Copper Shield: A full-coverage electrolytic copper screen inserted between the primary and secondary windings. Shunts common-mode noise, switching spikes, and high-frequency harmonics directly to ground, achieving over 60 dB noise attenuation.
  3. High-Permeability Grain-Oriented Silicon Steel Core: Built from low-loss cold-rolled grain-oriented (CRGO) silicon steel laminations with precision mitered step-lap joints, minimizing eddy current losses, excitation current, and acoustic humming.
  4. Vacuum Pressure Impregnation (VPI) Resin Coating: The complete core and coil assembly undergoes high-vacuum pressure impregnation with Class H ($180^\circ ext{C}$) solventless polymer resin, curing into a void-free, moisture-proof, and chemical-resistant monolithic block.
  5. Heavy-Duty Tinned Copper Terminal Blocks & Enclosure: High-conductivity terminal blocks supporting multi-conductor power cabling, housed within a ventilated NEMA 1 / IP20 to IP54 powder-coated steel cabinet equipped with lifting eyes and vibration-damping mounting pads.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an Isolation Transformer and how does Galvanic Isolation protect electrical equipment?

An Isolation Transformer—frequently designated as a galvanic isolation transformer, dry-type safety isolating transformer, or medical IT transformer—is a specialized stationary transformer whose primary (input) and secondary (output) winding coils are completely physically and magnetically coupled <strong>without any direct electrical or metallic connection</strong> between them. In modern electrical distribution and automation engineering: <ul><li><strong>Galvanic Decoupling &amp; Ground Loop Elimination:</strong> In standard utility electrical circuits, the neutral conductor is bonded to earth at the substation. When sensitive computer servers, CNC controllers, or medical laboratory instrumentation are connected to different ground rods across a facility, minute potential differences generate continuous circulating ground currents (ground loops), causing severe communication errors, measuring drift, and electronic component failure. An isolation transformer breaks the ground loop completely by creating a clean, ungrounded, freshly established secondary reference plane.</li><li><strong>Electric Shock Mitigation (Ungrounded Floating Secondary):</strong> Touching a live phase conductor on a standard grounded utility system allows electric current to flow through the human body back to earth, causing lethal electrocution. With an ungrounded 1:1 isolation transformer, touching a single secondary phase conductor does not complete an electrical circuit to earth, preventing electric shock in hazardous or wet operating environments.</li></ul>

What is an Electrostatic Faraday Shield and why is it essential for VFDs and sensitive electronics?

Standard transformers inherently possess inter-winding parasitic capacitance ($C_{ps}$) between adjacent primary and secondary coil layers. High-frequency voltage spikes, lightning impulses, and rapid switching transients ($dv/dt$) from Variable Frequency Drives (VFDs) easily bridge across this parasitic capacitance, passing directly through the transformer into delicate electronic microprocessors. YOMIN SG series transformers incorporate an <strong>Electrostatic Faraday Shield</strong>: <ul><li><strong>Structure:</strong> A continuous sheet of insulated electrolytic copper foil placed physically between the primary and secondary windings, deliberately left with a non-shorting overlap and connected directly to the transformer's low-impedance earth ground.</li><li><strong>Performance:</strong> It acts as an electrostatic barrier that diverts common-mode high-frequency noise currents straight into the ground before they can capacitively couple into the secondary winding, delivering over <strong>60 to 80 dB of common-mode noise attenuation</strong>.</li></ul>

What is the difference between an Autotransformer and an Isolation Transformer?

While both devices adjust voltage levels, their internal architectures and safety capabilities are completely opposite: <ul><li><strong>Autotransformer (Single Shared Winding):</strong> Utilizes a single continuous copper coil where the secondary voltage is tapped from the same winding as the primary. Because primary and secondary share a common metallic connection, autotransformers offer <strong>ZERO galvanic isolation</strong>, zero noise filtering, and pass grid surges, earth faults, and DC offsets straight through to downstream loads.</li><li><strong>Isolation Transformer (Dual Independent Windings):</strong> Features two completely separate, physically isolated coils wound on opposite sections of the core. It delivers 100% dielectric isolation, protects human operators from ground faults, and filters harmonics, making it mandatory for medical operating rooms, data center PDU cabinets, and precision automated machinery.</li></ul>
