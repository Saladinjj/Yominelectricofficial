What Is the Difference Between a Power Meter and an Energy Meter

ET
ET Engineering Team 7 min read August 20, 2026

An energy meter measures electrical energy consumed over time in kilowatt-hours (kWh), while a power meter measures electrical power parameters such as watts, voltage, current, and power factor. In practice the two terms are often used interchangeably, but understanding the distinction helps you choose the right instrument for billing, monitoring, or analysis.

What is a power meter?

A power meter is a broad term for an instrument that measures electrical power and related parameters. Basic power meters display watts, volts, amps, and power factor; advanced ones, often called power analyzers, also measure harmonics, frequency, and total harmonic distortion (THD).

What is an energy meter?

An energy meter (also called a kWh meter or watt-hour meter) measures the amount of electrical energy consumed over time and integrates it into kilowatt-hours. It is the device behind utility bills, submetering, and prepaid systems. Energy meters are certified for billing accuracy.

Key differences

FeaturePower meterEnergy meter
Primary measurementPower parameters (W, V, A, PF)Energy (kWh) over time
Typical outputInstant values and readingsRegisters and totals
Billing certifiedUsually notYes (MID, IEC 62053)
Best forMonitoring and analysisBilling and consumption tracking

Which one do you need?

  • For utility bills, tenant billing, or prepaid systems — choose an energy meter.
  • For live load monitoring, demand profiles, or troubleshooting — a power meter or analyzer fits better.
  • For high-current circuits — pair either with current transformers.

FAQ

Can an energy meter be called a power meter?

Yes, in everyday language they are often used interchangeably. Technically, an energy meter measures energy in kWh, while a power meter may measure power parameters such as watts, volts, amps, and power factor.

Which one do I need for billing?

Billing requires an energy meter that measures kWh accurately and meets certification standards such as MID or IEC 62053.

Do power analyzers count as power meters?

Power analyzers are advanced power meters that also measure power quality parameters such as harmonics and THD.

Final Thoughts

If you are measuring what you consume, choose an energy meter; if you are measuring how the power behaves, choose a power meter. Yomin Electric’s energy meter range covers both billing and monitoring needs with certified accuracy.
