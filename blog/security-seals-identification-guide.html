Meter Seals & Bolt Seals: Small Parts, Serious Security

ET Engineering Team 5 min read · September 1, 2026

A security seal is a promise you can see: if it's intact, nobody has been inside. Utilities seal meter terminals; logistics seal containers; customs seal trucks. The chart above shows the two seals we ship by the million — here's where each one earns its keep.

The 2 on the Chart

Wire meter seal (twist-tite, plastic body, cable wire)

The classic utility seal: a one-way plastic body that locks a stainless wire loop through the meter's terminal cover. Once tightened, it only comes off with cutters — and a cut seal is an instant red flag. Custom logos and sequential numbering make every seal traceable to a route or technician.

High security bolt seal (PM-BS9201)

A hardened steel bolt and cylinder that snaps together once and breaks apart only with bolt cutters. The standard for containers, tankers and trailer doors in transit — the seal insurers and customs authorities expect on high-value cargo.

Which Seal for Which Job?

Ordering Tips

  • Put your logo and sequential numbers on the seal — unmarked seals are easy to swap; yours shouldn't be.
  • Choose colours per route or region for instant visual control.
  • For cargo, specify high-security bolt seals and keep the numbering log with the shipment documents.

FAQ

Can seals be reused?

No — both types are single-use by design. Any attempt to open them leaves obvious damage.

What's the MOQ for custom logo seals?

Custom printing has a modest MOQ that scales down the per-unit price fast — send your artwork and target quantity.

Do bolt seals meet international standards?

Our high-security bolt seals are produced for ISO 17712-type applications — confirm the exact standard you need with the order.
