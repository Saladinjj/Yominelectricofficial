Factory Energy Monitoring: Cut Costs with Real-Time Data

ET
ET Engineering Team 7 min read July 28, 2026

Most factories cannot say exactly where their electricity goes. Energy monitoring changes that by putting a meter on every production line and watching the numbers in real time.

Start with Submetering

Install DIN rail meters on each panel, line, or department. RS485 or Modbus communication consolidates every meter into one dashboard, revealing which lines consume the most and when.

What Real-Time Data Reveals

Idle machines left running, compressed air leaks, off-hours baseload, and equipment drifting out of efficiency all appear as data patterns. Most plants find 10–20% savings just by acting on what they see.

Power Quality Matters Too

Monitor voltage, current and power factor at key nodes. Low power factor means paying for reactive energy; correcting it with capacitors can cut demand charges immediately.

Building the Monitoring Stack

Meters with Modbus output + a gateway + dashboards are the standard recipe. Start with the top ten loads, publish a weekly report, and let departments compete to improve their numbers.

Conclusion

You cannot manage what you do not measure. A submetering system pays for itself in months and keeps paying as your plant grows.
