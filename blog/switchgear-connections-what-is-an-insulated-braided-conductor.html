Flexible Busbars & Power Connections / Insulated Braided Conductors

Switchgear Connections: What Is an Insulated Braided Conductor?

ET
ET Engineering Team October 16, 2026 10 min read

The Ultimate Flexible Power Bridge for Modern Electrical Distribution Switchgear

Inside modern low-voltage electrical switchboards, main distribution switchgear, transformer terminal enclosures, and industrial motor control centers (MCC) carrying currents from 100A to 1000A, connecting heavy electrical apparatus in confined spaces presents severe engineering challenges.

Connecting a molded case circuit breaker (MCCB) or air circuit breaker (ACB) to the main horizontal busbar using rigid copper busbars requires complex three-dimensional CAD modeling, costly hydraulic cutting and bending machinery, and millimeter-precise manual fitting. Any structural misalignment during panel assembly exerts dangerous mechanical stress on the circuit breaker terminal stabs.

Conversely, utilizing standard insulated power cables requires stripping outer sheaths, crimping terminal lugs, and providing generous bending radiuses that consume excessive cabinet depth and clutter cable gutters.

The Pre-Insulated Flexible Braided Conductor (Model IBS / IBSB Series) provides the universal high-current engineering solution: a ready-to-install, highly flexible woven tinned copper braid encased in an extruded high-dielectric vinyl jacket, featuring solid forged connection palms that bolt directly to breaker terminals with zero crimped lugs and zero specialized tooling.

Engineering Anatomy: Inside a Model IBSB Insulated Braided Conductor

  1. High-Purity Electrolytic Tinned Copper Braid Core: Woven from thousands of micro-fine ($0.15$ to $0.20\, ext{mm}$) Cu-ETP 99.9% pure copper strands. Tin plating provides superior surface oxidation resistance, preventing galvanic corrosion when bolted to aluminum or tin-plated busbars.
  2. Monolithic Solid Forged Connection Palms: Both connection ends are processed through molecular diffusion bonding and high-tonnage precision forging, transforming the loose braided wires into a solid, seamless copper block. Pre-punched with standard bolt holes (M6, M8, M10, or M12) for direct bolting.
  3. High-Dielectric Extruded Vinyl Insulation Sleeve: Formulated from self-extinguishing, flame-retardant PVC/LSZH compound (UL 94 V-0). Extruded directly over the braid with uniform wall thickness, delivering 1000V AC / 1500V DC working dielectric isolation with high resistance to oils and acids.
  4. Multi-Axis Three-Dimensional Flexibility: Unlike laminated busbars that only bend along a single perpendicular axis, the woven braided structure flexes freely in all three spatial dimensions, twisting and bending around cabinet obstructions with minimal bend radiuses.
  5. Superior Vibration & Thermal Expansion Damping: Absorbs severe high-frequency acoustic vibrations and mechanical shocks from operating power transformers, diesel generators, and heavy motor drives, preventing bolted joints from working loose.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an Insulated Braided Conductor (IBS / IBSB) and what problems does it solve?

An Insulated Braided Conductor—frequently designated as an IBSB flexible braided shunt, pre-insulated copper braid strap, or flexible power bridge—is a specialized electrical conductor engineered from thousands of interwoven high-purity electrolytic tinned copper strands, encased in an extruded flame-retardant insulating jacket, with its connection ends formed into <strong>seamless solid copper palms</strong> with pre-punched bolt holes. In electrical power switchboards and motor control centers: <ul><li><strong>The Limits of Rigid Busbars:</strong> Rigid solid copper busbars cannot absorb mechanical vibrations from transformers, generators, or large AC motors. Over time, continuous harmonic vibration loosens bolted joints, creating high electrical contact resistance, overheating, and catastrophic arc-flash fires. Furthermore, aligning rigid busbars in tight enclosures requires expensive, labor-intensive precision bending machines.</li><li><strong>The Limits of Power Cables:</strong> Standard flexible power cables require large bending radiuses, taking up massive cabinet depth. Linemen must strip the cable, slip on a crimp lug, and hydraulically compress it—creating two potential mechanical failure interfaces. The YOMIN IBSB series solves both issues by combining the flexibility of woven copper braid with the current density of solid busbars, bending effortlessly in tight spaces with zero mechanical crimp lugs.</li></ul>

How do Integral Solid Forged Palms eliminate electrical failure compared to crimped terminal lugs?

Conventional braided ground straps rely on crimped metal terminal lugs stamped onto the ends of the braid. Under thermal cycling (heating during peak factory shifts and cooling during nights), differential thermal expansion creates micro-gaps between the braid wires and the crimped lug sleeve. Moisture and airborne sulfur enter these voids, forming non-conductive copper oxide that increases joint resistance and triggers thermal runaway. YOMIN IBSB conductors feature <strong>integral solid connection palms forged directly from the copper braid strands</strong> through high-pressure diffusion forging. The connection palm becomes a monolithic, solid piece of 100% pure copper with zero internal voids and zero crimped interfaces, guaranteeing permanent low contact resistance and identical conductivity to solid drawn busbars.
