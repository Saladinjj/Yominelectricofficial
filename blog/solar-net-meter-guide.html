Solar Net Meter: How Net Metering Works & How to Choose One

ET Engineering Team 7 min read · September 1, 2026

Direct answer: a solar net meter measures both the energy you draw from the grid and the energy your solar system exports back. Under net metering, the utility bills you on the difference — so the meter has to run in both directions, or the utility installs a dedicated bidirectional net meter at the service point.

Net metering programs are expanding fast in South Asia (Pakistan 2.9K monthly searches, India 2.4K), the Philippines and beyond. If you're an installer, distributor or utility buyer, understanding how the net meter fits into the scheme decides what you order.

How Net Metering Works

  1. Your solar array generates power; the inverter feeds your loads first.
  2. Surplus energy exports to the grid — the meter records it as export.
  3. At night, you import from the grid — the meter records it as import.
  4. The utility nets import minus export and bills the difference (or credits it).

What Makes a Meter "Net" or "Bidirectional"

FeatureStandard meterBidirectional / net meter
DirectionOne-way (import only)Import + export
RegistersSingle kWh registerSeparate import/export registers (or net register)
Used forResidential consumptionSolar/grid-tie installations, net billing

Our DIN-rail active net metering meter (YEM015SD) is the small footprint option for single-phase solar installs, while the bidirectional electronic meter handles two-way measurement for larger systems. Some utilities accept a bidirectional meter with separate registers; others require a specific net meter model — check your local DISCOM/utility list first.

What to Check Before Buying

  • Utility approval list: many programs only accept approved meter models — verify before you order.
  • Register configuration: separate import/export registers vs a single net register.
  • Communication: RS485 or optical port if the utility reads remotely.
  • Accuracy & standards: IEC 62053 compliance is the usual baseline.
  • Mounting: DIN rail vs panel — DIN rail suits compact residential installs.

FAQ

Can a normal meter run backwards with solar?

Mechanical meters can run backwards — which is exactly why utilities won't allow it. A net meter records export separately instead of reversing.

Do I need a net meter or a bidirectional meter?

It depends on the utility's scheme. "Net metering" often uses a bidirectional meter with separate registers; "net billing" may need a smart meter. Confirm the approved list in your market.
